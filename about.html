Our Story

Keeping Traditional Craft Alive, One Piece At A Time

CraftNest was born from a simple belief — that handmade objects carry warmth, memory, and meaning that mass-produced goods never can. We partner with master artisans across India to bring their craft to homes worldwide.

Our Story

From A Small Workshop To A Global Craft Family

What started in 2001 as a small workshop in Jaipur — with just three artisans and a single pottery wheel — has grown into a family of over 180 makers across India. But our mission has never changed.

We work directly with artisans, pay them fairly, and give them a platform to share their craft with the world. Every piece you buy from CraftNest carries a real story and supports a real family.

"We don't just sell handicrafts. We keep traditions alive — one handmade piece, one artisan family at a time."

Our Mission & Vision

Two pillars guide everything we do — from the artisans we work with to the homes we deliver to.

Our Mission

To preserve traditional handicraft techniques by connecting skilled artisans with conscious buyers — ensuring fair pay, sustainable materials, and craft that lasts for generations. We believe every handmade piece should tell a story worth passing down.

Our Vision

A world where handmade is not a luxury — but the everyday choice. Where every home has at least one piece that was shaped by human hands, carries a memory, and connects its owner to the maker behind it.

Our Journey

Two decades of craft, growth, and community — a milestone at every turn.

2001

The First Workshop

Started in Jaipur with 3 artisans, one pottery wheel, and a small shop selling hand-thrown vases.

2008

Artisan Network Grows

Partnered with 25 more artisans across pottery, woodwork, and textiles — expanding our handmade family.

2015

Going Global

Launched our first online store and began shipping handmade crafts to homes in the USA, UK, and Europe.

2020

Sustainable Shift

Moved to 100% plastic-free packaging and introduced natural, non-toxic finishes across all products.

2026

A Family Of 180+

Today we work with 180+ artisans across 12 states — serving 40,000+ homes worldwide with handmade craft.
